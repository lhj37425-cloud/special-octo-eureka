Privacy policy

How we use your inquiry information

This notice explains how EVOL DREAM handles website inquiries.

Information collected

The form collects your name, company, contact details, selected service and optional description. It also records the source page, language, campaign parameters, submission time and consent version to save and follow up on your inquiry.

Storage and use

Inquiry details are stored in a database on our Singapore server and sent to our designated business inbox through Alibaba Cloud DirectMail. We do not send inquiry details to AI models or use advertising trackers at present.

Browser session storage

The site may use session storage for campaign parameters and your latest reference number to display a confirmation. The browser clears this when the session ends. Your name and contact details are not stored there.

Access, correction and deletion

To access, correct or delete your inquiry, email your reference number to ezra.y.franklin@gmail.com

Please avoid sensitive information

Employee CVs, identity documents, passwords and customer data are not needed for an initial inquiry. Share only what is necessary to explain the business problem.
