Training goals

Four core capabilities

Not just learning the tools, but finding where AI fits in your own role and keeping it in steady use.

  • Applying AI

    Use AI tools confidently to organize materials, generate content and analyze information.

  • Understanding the business

    Break role tasks down clearly and judge which steps suit AI.

  • Designing use cases

    Prepare materials, design use cases, and build agents and workflows.

  • Delivering projects

    Check AI output and handle exceptions so applications keep running in daily work.

Who it is for

Two programs for two needs

Develop new AI application talent, or strengthen the team you already have.

AI application and delivery talent

For: students, engineers, business service staff and anyone who wants to work in AI applications

  • Learn and practice through business tasks, from understanding needs and preparing data to building and accepting an application.
  • Produce work that can be demonstrated, explained and checked.
  • Cases cover manufacturing, trade, logistics, retail and services.
Practice with real tasks

In-house team training

  • Training designed around the company’s own processes.

  • Build a team that can identify needs, use the tools and maintain applications.
Assessed by work results
