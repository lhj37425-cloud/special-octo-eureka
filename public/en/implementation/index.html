FDE delivery method

Seven delivery steps

  1. 01

    Business assessment

    Understand goals, processes and roles; identify repetitive work, scattered information and coordination problems.

  2. 02

    Application design

    Choose a pilot task by business value and delivery conditions; define the scope, deliverables and acceptance method.

  3. 03

    Data and knowledge preparation

    Organize materials, knowledge and data sources; check completeness, usability and access rights.

  4. 04

    Build agents and workflows

    Build AI agents for each task, connect the necessary tools and systems, and set processing steps and human confirmation.

  5. 05

    Pilot operation

    Use it within a limited scope; track result quality, processing time and usage; record errors and exceptions.

  6. 06

    Continuous optimization

    Adjust materials, rules and workflows based on operating data and business feedback.

  7. 07

    Training and handover

    Hand over user guides, maintenance methods and related materials; assign internal owners and a follow-up support plan.

Handover criteria

  • Staff can use it
  • Results can be checked
  • The application can be maintained
  • Impact can be measured

From pilot to continuous operation

Helping companies use, maintain and gradually expand AI applications

Once an application is in use, someone needs to update materials, check results and handle changes. EVOL DREAM combines training with delivery, building usage and maintenance methods during the pilot, then expanding applications based on actual results.

Continuous improvement inside the company

Start from one business problem and keep tracking results and usage.

  • PeopleAssign business owners, users and maintainers, and organize the training they need.
  • MaterialsUpdate product information, business rules and operating requirements.
  • ApplicationMonitor operation and record errors, exceptions and improvement needs.
  • ProcessAdjust work steps and review methods as the business changes.
  • EvaluationCompare processing time, result quality and workload before and after delivery.
