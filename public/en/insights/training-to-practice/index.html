Insights / Skills development

Make AI training part of everyday work

Bring real tasks into the classroom, then take the results back into your workflow.

Build the course around job tasks

Leaders, sales teams, HR and technical staff face different challenges, even within the same company. Collect recurring tasks and sample materials before training to connect exercises to each role.

Give participants something they can use

A use case assessment, a reviewed content draft or a working workflow is easier to keep using at work than a list of tool names.

Practice reviewing AI output

Training should cover more than generation. Practice spotting missing information, factual errors and unsuitable wording. Keep human approval where outputs affect external communications or business decisions.

Review what happens back at work

Identify team members willing to keep using the tools and record the issues they encounter. Use their feedback to improve materials and workflows, then decide whether to run an agent pilot.
